Guidebook theme: decision, tension held in balance, careful pause. Visual meditation: move between gate and path; notice distance and exchange.

### Prompt

Create a new vertical contemplative tarot-card artwork. At first light beside a shallow river, one unidentified adult traveler pauses on a broad flat stone where two currents meet and become one. Their body is naturally still but not rigid: one foot remains on the bank, the other tests the wet stone; one hand holds a plain walking staff lowered toward the water, the other is open. A single cloth bundle rests behind them, small and uncounted. The two currents reflect cool dawn and warm sky, making a quiet tension without a literal crossroads sign or pair of objects. The emotional event is a careful pause before an ethically consequential choice, with dignity, uncertainty, and enough space to listen. Use restrained garden vegetation, wind in reeds, and a distant path that disappears into mist. One person only, face in soft three-quarter profile, anatomically natural hands, no dramatic gesture. Keep architecture absent or only a barely visible low stone edge; nature and the person's posture carry the meaning. Finish with the shared portrait border and blank lower cartouche; no text.

Card-specific avoid: two gates, two swords, two identical objects, scales, a literal forked-road diagram, chessboard, courtroom, posed decision gesture, fantasy glow.
